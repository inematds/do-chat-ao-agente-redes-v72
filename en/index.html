Course · step 4 of the From chat to agent program · 8 lessons

Social media on autopilot, with the source on screen

A 7-step reel, videos by audience, and a channel for your niche that watches the biggest ones, checks every number, and only publishes what you approved. In eight lessons you build the whole pipeline: rules, a base with sources, a checked script, a schedule, and a gate.

The lessons

In pipeline order: first the script, then the rules and the base with sources, the checked script, the videos by audience, and finally the schedule with a gate.

  1. Lesson 1 · 15 minThe queue line of a channel with an on-screen sourceThe finviral, saudeviral, and advviral radars automatically make one video a day about what the biggest creators in the niche have published, with the source of each number on screen. r7e and promoavatar make the reel and the videos for each audience. Before you build your own, look at the whole queue line and the role of each tool.
  2. Lesson 2 · 18 minThe reel in 7 stages: plan before speechThe r7e gives you the script: seven trigger parts, from a hook with a number to a request for comments. It took four batches of reels to reach the r7ex pattern: the content got right when the plan came before the spoken lines, and the form got right with a TV-news finish. Here you learn the plan, the seven parts, and the rules for the spoken lines.
  3. Lesson 3 · 16 minThe 5 in your niche and the rules that are never brokenA radar is only as good as the sources it watches and the rules it obeys. Finance, health, and law use the same engine, but each niche has its own required disclaimer, its own definition of what counts as a fact, and its own list of what never goes on air. In this lesson you choose your references and write the rules for your niche.
  4. Lesson 4 · 18 minThe sourced base: every number with the literal excerptThe base.md is the heart of the channel with a source. Every number that goes on screen goes in with who said it, the video, the date, and an excerpt copied from the transcript, unedited. A script checks each excerpt; if any is not there, the video is discarded. Here you build your own base and your own checker.
  5. Lesson 5 · 17 min60-second script with the source on screenWith the base ready, the agent writes the script, and only from what is in the base. The radar opens with the date and the strongest number, covers each topic with the profile card of who said it, and ends with a question and the invitation. Before it becomes a video, the script goes through the check: if a number isn't in the base, the script goes back.
  6. Lesson 6 · 17 minOne topic, many audiences: promoavatar and promoavatar3The same topic doesn't persuade a young person and a retiree in the same way. promoavatar writes one script for each of 12 audiences; promoavatar3 writes three for the same audience: reach, authority, and promotional. In both, the bot writes and stops. You approve before spending avatar and render.
  7. Lesson 7 · 18 minSchedule with a gate: the channel runs on its own, you decideThe schedule turns the pipeline into a channel: one run a day, at a fixed time, without you having to remember. But a schedule without a gate just posts the wrong thing faster. In this lesson you write the timer, decide what happens on a day with no topic, and run a week approving every video, until the rejections stop.
  8. Lesson 8 · 12 minYour channel, in the right orderYou've seen the whole pipeline. Now put the pieces together in the order they depend on each other: the reel on paper, the rules, the base, the checked script, the schedule and the week with the gate. Generate your plan with dates and go back to the course overview to see how this step rests on the other three.

Who it's for

  1. 1People who have already done steps 1 to 3 (terminal, your own assistant, and the queue) and want to take this to social media.
  2. 2Professionals and creators in a niche who want to publish regularly without making up numbers.
  3. 3People who guide teams or clients and need a method for automated content done responsibly.

What you'll be able to do

  1. 1Write a 7-step reel from a plan with a potential score.
  2. 2Build canais.md, regras.md, and base.md for your niche, with the literal excerpt for each number.
  3. 3Ask the agent for a 60 s script and send back whatever doesn't match the base.
  4. 4Write the schedule (timer) and run a week with a gate, turning each refusal into a rule.
