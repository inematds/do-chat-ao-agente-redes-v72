Lesson 3 · 16 min

The 5 in your niche and the rules that never change

A radar is only as good as the sources it watches and the rules it follows. Finance, health, and law use the same engine, but each niche has its own required disclaimer, what counts as a fact, and what never goes live. In this lesson, you’ll choose your references and write the rules for your niche.

Start the lesson

In this lesson, you will

  1. 1How to choose references (and who never makes the cut)
  2. 2The four parts of regras.md
  3. 3How finance, health, and law each handled it
  4. 4How a rejection becomes a new rule

Goal: create canais.md and regras.md for your niche: 5 established references, what counts as a fact, the required disclaimer, and what never gets published.

02 · Three niches, three ways to protect viewers

Real radar rules. Use them as a starting point for your own.

1. Finance

References: the Anbima FInfluence ranking’s Top 10. Disclaimer: "This is not investment advice."

Elections and politics: the video reports what each person said, always attributed and without taking sides.

RuleOnly include numbers from the transcript, with the source (channel, date, title) on screen.

2. Health

References: the most widely followed doctors, nutritionists, and psychologists, based on a dated search.

Anything without scientific consensus gets the NO CONSENSUS label or is left out. Channels with this profile stay active at the owner’s discretion, always with the label.

RuleNo dosage, medication, or diet instructions. "This does not replace a medical consultation." in every video.

3. Law

References: 10 YouTube lawyers by practice area (social security, consumer rights, debt, employment, real estate, family, scams). Channels teaching for civil service exams were left out.

Nothing becomes advice for the viewer’s own situation.

Rule"Every case is different: consult a lawyer or the public defender’s office."

4. Your niche

Ask: what could harm someone watching if it’s said incorrectly in my niche? The answer becomes the disclaimer and the list of things I never publish.

Choose 5 channels using criteria you can write in one line.

Real estateDisclaimer: "this is not an appraisal of your property." I never publish: a specific property’s price as a certainty.

Repository example

Across the three radars, if another established and trustworthy name appears in the spotlight, the system suggests adding it in the bot’s message. Emerging names are never added, and additions only happen with the owner’s OK.

In health, the criteria also require professional registration.

rk appears on the influencer’s card in the video: "No. 1 · ANBIMA" in finviral, "DOCTOR · ONCOLOGIST" in saudeviral, "LAW · SOCIAL SECURITY" in advviral. Viewers can see why that person is a source.
