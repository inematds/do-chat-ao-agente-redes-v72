Lesson 3 · 16 min

The 5 in your niche and the rules that are never broken

A radar is only as good as the sources it watches and the rules it obeys. Finance, health, and law use the same engine, but each niche has its own required disclaimer, its own definition of what counts as a fact, and its own list of what never goes on air. In this lesson you choose your references and write the rules for your niche.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1How to choose references (and who never gets in)
  2. 2The four parts of regras.md
  3. 3How finance, health, and law each solved each part
  4. 4How a refusal becomes a new rule

Goal: build canais.md and regras.md for your niche: 5 consolidated references, what counts as a fact, the required disclaimer, and what is never published.

02 · Three niches, three ways to protect the viewer

Real rules from the radars. Use them as a starting point for yours.

1. Finance

References: the Top 10 of the Anbima FInfluence ranking. Disclaimer: "not investment advice".

Elections and politics: the video reports what each person said, always attributed, without taking sides.

RuleOnly numbers that are in the transcript go in, with the source (channel, date, title) on screen.

2. Health

References: the most-watched doctors, nutritionists and psychologists, from a dated survey.

Anything without scientific consensus gets the NO CONSENSUS stamp or stays out. Channels with this profile stay active by the owner's decision, always with the stamp.

RuleNo dose, medication or diet as instruction. "Does not replace a medical consultation." in every video.

3. Law

References: 10 YouTube lawyers by area (social security, consumer, debts, labor, real estate, family, scams). Channels for civil-service exam prep were left out.

Nothing becomes advice for the viewer's own case.

Rule"Every case is different: talk to a lawyer or the public defender's office."

4. Your niche

Ask: what, in my niche, could harm the viewer if said wrongly? The answer becomes the disclaimer and the list of what I never publish.

Pick 5 channels using a criterion you can write in one line.

Real estateDisclaimer: "not an appraisal of your property". I never publish: the price of a specific property as certain.

Example from the repository

In all three radars, if another established and trustworthy name shows up as a highlight, the system suggests adding it in the bot message. Emerging names never get in, and a name is only added with the owner's OK.

In health, the criterion also requires a professional license.

The rk appears on the influencer's card in the video: "No. 1 · ANBIMA" in finviral, "DOCTOR · ONCOLOGIST" in saudeviral, "LAW · SOCIAL SECURITY" in advviral. Viewers see why that person is a reference.
