Lesson 6 · 17 min

One topic, different audiences: promoavatar and promoavatar3

The same topic won’t persuade a young person and a retiree in the same way. promoavatar writes a script for each of 12 audiences; promoavatar3 writes three for the same audience: reach, authority, and promotional. In both, the bot writes and stops. You approve before spending on the avatar and rendering.

Start the lesson

In this lesson, you will

  1. 1The 12 audiences and what motivates each one
  2. 2Reach, authority, and promotional
  3. 3The two gates: before the avatar and before rendering
  4. 4Why the video title is a contract

Goal: plan 3 videos for the same audience (reach, authority, promotional) with the two gates and each avatar’s contract title.

01 · The promoavatar cycle

Four phases and two gates. The pipeline stops at the gates and only moves forward when you approve.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

Approving first is better than retrying later: avatar and render cost money and can’t be undone. The first gate protects your avatar spend; the second protects your render spend.

02 · Three videos for the same audience (promoavatar3)

Publish in this order: reach → authority → promotional.

1. Reach

Goal: stop the scroll and get shared.

CTA: comment or share. Never promotional.

Studio titleC7-jovens-alc-v1

2. Authority

Goal: teach something concrete and get saved.

CTA: save, follow, or try it.

Studio titleC7-jovens-aut-v1

3. Promotional

Goal: connect the pain point to the solution.

CTA: just one, for inema.club.

Studio titleC7-jovens-pro-v1

4. The 12 audiences

everyday people, young people, professionals, women, entrepreneurs, technicians, 40+, 60+, educators, creators, people looking for a new job, and families. Each audience has its own trigger and channel.

03 · Commands and contract

Bot commands (inemaccbot). For your channel, the key pattern is: filter, review the plan, approve.

bot chat
# review the plan without spending anything (does not add to the queue)
/promoavatar3 <assunto> | alvos=jovens-alc,jovens-aut,jovens-pro | sombra

# run for just one audience, across all three types
/promoavatar3 <assunto> | alvos=jovens-alc,jovens-aut,jovens-pro

# after reviewing the text
/aprovar C#7
titulo-contrato.txt
promoavatar:   A<N>-<publico>-v1        ex.: A8-mulheres-v1
promoavatar3:  C<N>-<publico>-<tipo>-v1 ex.: C7-jovens-pro-v1

The download matches by exact equality. Short suffixes (alc, aut, pro):

  • promoavatar3: three videos per audience (reach, authority, promo), 36 possible targets. Reference C#N. The usual way to filter is | alvos=.
  • They are separate systems: changing one does not affect the other.

| prompt=viral: reach above all. No brand, no course, and no inema.club in the spoken lines; always use an engagement CTA, with a flexible length of 15 to 45 s. The final clip shows only the brand, so it does not compete with the request to comment.
