Lesson 6 · 17 min

One topic, many audiences: promoavatar and promoavatar3

The same topic doesn't persuade a young person and a retiree in the same way. promoavatar writes one script for each of 12 audiences; promoavatar3 writes three for the same audience: reach, authority, and promotional. In both, the bot writes and stops. You approve before spending avatar and render.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The 12 audiences and the trigger for each
  2. 2Reach, authority, and promotional
  3. 3The two gates: before the avatar and before the render
  4. 4Why the video title is a contract

Goal: plan 3 videos for the same audience (reach, authority, promotional) with the two gates and the contract title of each avatar.

01 · The promoavatar cycle

Four phases and two gates. The pipeline stops on its own at the gates and only moves on when you release it.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

Approving first is worth more than retrying later: avatar and render cost money and can't be undone. The first gate protects the avatar spend; the second protects the render spend.

02 · Three videos for the same audience (promoavatar3)

Publish in this order: reach → authority → promotional.

1. Reach

Goal: stop the scroll and get shared.

CTA: comment or share. Never commercial.

Title in the studioC7-jovens-alc-v1

2. Authority

Goal: teach something concrete and get saved.

CTA: save, follow, or try it.

Title in the studioC7-jovens-aut-v1

3. Promotional

Goal: connect the pain to the solution.

CTA: just one, to inema.club.

Title in the studioC7-jovens-pro-v1

4. The 12 audiences

everyday person, young people, professionals, women, entrepreneurs, technicians, 40+, 60+, educators, creators, career restart, and family. Each audience has its own trigger and its own channel.

40+ triggerYour experience is worth more when AI multiplies it.

03 · The commands and the contract

Bot commands (inemaccbot). For your channel, what matters is the pattern: filter, see the plan, approve.

bot chat
# see the plan without spending anything (does not queue)
/promoavatar3 <assunto> | alvos=jovens-alc,jovens-aut,jovens-pro | sombra

# run for just one audience, across all three types
/promoavatar3 <assunto> | alvos=jovens-alc,jovens-aut,jovens-pro

# after reviewing the texts
/aprovar C#7
titulo-contrato.txt
promoavatar:   A<N>-<publico>-v1        ex.: A8-mulheres-v1
promoavatar3:  C<N>-<publico>-<tipo>-v1 ex.: C7-jovens-pro-v1

Download matches by exact equality. Short suffixes (alc, aut, pro):
a long title was already truncated in the studio and broke the match.

Example from the repository
  • promoavatar: one video per audience, 12 audiences. Reference A#N.
  • promoavatar3: three videos per audience (alc, aut, pro), 36 possible targets. Reference C#N. The usual approach is to filter with | alvos=.
  • They are separate systems: changing one doesn't affect the other.

| prompt=viral: reach above all. No brand, no course, and no inema.club in the script; the CTA is always about engagement, with free length from 15 to 45 s. The final clip becomes just the brand, so it doesn't compete with the request to comment.
