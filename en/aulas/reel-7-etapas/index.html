Lesson 2 · 18 min

The reel in 7 stages: plan before speech

The r7e gives you the script: seven trigger parts, from a hook with a number to a request for comments. It took four batches of reels to reach the r7ex pattern: the content got right when the plan came before the spoken lines, and the form got right with a TV-news finish. Here you learn the plan, the seven parts, and the rules for the spoken lines.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The plan in five fields, before writing the spoken lines
  2. 2The seven parts, with the real example from the hacker reel
  3. 3The rules for the spoken lines: length, numbers spelled out, CTA
  4. 4What the four batches taught

Goal: write a 30 to 45 s reel in 7 steps from a plan with audience, desire, hook with a number, score, and destination.

    No avatar or render before the plan and the sheet gate. What costs money (avatar, images, render) only starts after the cheap part (text) has passed.

02 · The 7 parts, in the hacker reel

Real example from r7ex (published reel). The order of parts 2 to 6 adapts to the topic; it is always seven parts of ~30–40 s.

1. Hook

Fear, novelty, or provocation, with a number or fact. Never defuse the trigger in the same sentence.

Spoken lineHackers broke into OpenCode… US$ 1,700 in one night.

2. Fear or proof

Show why this hits the people watching. It can also be proof: "it has already happened 3 times".

Spoken lineThe key is the password the AI uses… yours could be next.

3. Desire

The person pictures themselves on the other side of the problem.

Spoken lineImagine sleeping soundly knowing that… the loss has a cap.

4. Ease

Answers the "I'm not technical" or "I don't have time" before the person scrolls away.

Spoken lineYou don't need to be an expert, or a programmer.

5. Discovery

This is where the name of the solution or the method goes, preferably in three items.

Spoken lineThere are three adjustments: a limit, one key per use, an alarm…

6. Benefit

What life looks like afterward, or the "take advantage before…".

Spoken lineThe scare turns into a notification on your phone…

7. Comment

The fixed closing leads to inema.club and asks for a comment. In the reel7etapas the request is "comment WORD"; in the r7ex, "ask me for the link".

Spoken lineIt's all on inema dot club. Or ask me for the link here in the comments.

request to the agent
Based on the plano.md below, write the speech for a reel in 7 parts
(hook with the number, fear or proof, desire, ease, discovery with
3 items, benefit, comment). Rules:
- 550 to 700 characters in total;
- numbers and years written out in words in the speech;
- no part ends on a loose number;
- do not make up numbers: use only what is in the plan, with the source;
- the first sentence never starts with "Imagine", "Hello" or "In this video".

  • Batch 1 (hook = promise, blurred photo): no views on TikTok or Instagram.
  • Batch 2 (only the hook became a trigger with a number): better, "still average".
  • Batch 3 (plan before the script, strong theses, fixed CTA, real screenshots): the content hit the mark.
  • Batch 4, r7ex (same script, newscast-style finish): the form hit the mark. Standard since 10/18/2026.

Script of 550 to 700 characters. Numbers and years written out in words, because the avatar reads them; the caption swaps them for digits. Never make up a number; "free" only when the page says so.

The plan targets a general audience (AI translated into everyday life), not the niche, because the goal of the reel is to bring in new people.

Exercise 1 of sequence 4.

E1 · A 7-step reel, on paper

20 min

Fill in the plano.md and write the 30-to-45-second reel for your niche using the 7-part model. Read it aloud and time yourself.

Done when: you read it aloud in under 45 s, the plan score is 7 or higher, and the first sentence already hooks (in the r7ex standard: the trigger with a number or fact).

The original exercise list says "the first sentence already states the gain". The r7ex standard (10/18/2026) swapped the gain for the trigger with a number in the hook, after batches with no views. Exercises: jornada-agentes/exercicios/04-redes.md.
