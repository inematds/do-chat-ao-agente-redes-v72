Lesson 1 · 15 min

A channel pipeline with sources on screen

The finviral, saudeviral, and advviral radar tools automatically make one video a day about what the biggest names in the niche have published, with the source for every number on screen. r7e and promoavatar make reels and videos for each audience. Before you build your own, see the whole pipeline and what each tool does.

Scroll down: each step below lights up its part of the diagram.

  1. 1/7

    Collection

    Lists videos from the channels in canais.json (metadata only, using yt-dlp), downloads the audio, and transcribes it on your own computer with inemavox (Whisper large-v3). No paid API is used at this step.

  2. 2/7

    Sourced database

    The agent reads the transcripts and records each number or fact with who said it, the video ID, the date, and the exact transcript excerpt. This is the only source material the script can use.

  3. 3/7

    Script

    Using your subscription and no API, claude -p writes the lines and visuals to roteiro.json and the approval text to legenda.txt, always based on the database.

  4. 4/7

    Review

    checar.py checks whether each excerpt from the database is actually in the transcript, whether the numbers in the spoken lines are spelled out, and whether the niche disclaimer and CTA appear. If it fails, the script gets one revision. If it fails again, the video is not made.

  5. 5/7

    Avatar and video

    Nei's avatar comes from the HeyGen studio (subscription). If that fails, the fallback is a version with a photo and local voice. The stage is a page rendered frame by frame, with counters, word-by-word captions, and the source on screen in every scene.

  6. 6/7

    Publishing

    The video goes to the YouTube channel through yt-pubx and always to the Telegram bot. The bot receives one of three things: the video, "no topic" (a slow day doesn't become a video), or "failed at X".

  7. 7/7

    The full pipeline

    Collection and transcription feed the database; the database constrains the script; review blocks anything without a source; the avatar and stage assemble the video; publishing always lets you know. Each station is a lesson in this course.

Without a queue and a gate, automating social media means posting the wrong thing faster. That's why this is step 4: it uses the terminal, assistant, and queue from the previous steps.

02 · What each tool does

Click each one. They are all open repositories from INEMA.

1. r7e and r7ex

You provide a strong idea, and get a 30 to 40 second 9:16 reel using the 7-step trigger script, from the number hook to the request for a comment.

r7e (reel7etapas) provided the script; r7ex is the new standard (10/18/2026), with a TV news finish. Lesson 2 of this course.

Input → outputOne idea for the day → one reel with an avatar, panels timed to the spoken lines, and "comment WORD" at the end.

2. promoavatar

One topic becomes a script for each of 12 audiences (young people, adults 40+, entrepreneurs, educators…), each with its own trigger and channel.

The bot writes and stops: you approve before spending on the avatar and rendering. Lesson 6.

Input → output"/promoavatar " → 12 scripts, 2 gates, 12 reels delivered to each audience’s channel.

3. promoavatar3

The same topic yields three scripts for the same audience: reach (to be shared), authority (to be saved), and promotional (to connect a pain point to a solution).

Recommended publishing order: reach → authority → promotional. Lesson 6.

Input → output"| alvos=jovens-alc,jovens-aut,jovens-pro" → three videos for young people, with different CTAs.

4. The radars (xviral)

They watch what the biggest names in the niche have published, check every number against the transcript, and publish one short video a day, with the source on screen and the niche disclaimer.

Finance, health, and law use the same engine; only the channel list, rules, and schedule change. Lessons 3, 4, 5, and 7.

Input → outputMonday-to-Friday timer → a 40- to 60-second video about what they said yesterday and the day before, or "no topic".
