Lesson 1 · 15 min

The queue line of a channel with an on-screen source

The finviral, saudeviral, and advviral radars automatically make one video a day about what the biggest creators in the niche have published, with the source of each number on screen. r7e and promoavatar make the reel and the videos for each audience. Before you build your own, look at the whole queue line and the role of each tool.

Scroll the page: each step below lights up its part of the diagram.

  1. 1/7

    Collection

    Lists the videos from the channels in canais.json (metadata only, through yt-dlp), downloads the audio, and transcribes it on your own computer with inemavox (Whisper large-v3). No paid API in this step.

  2. 2/7

    Source base

    The agent reads the transcripts and notes each number or fact with who said it, the video id, the date, and the verbatim excerpt from the transcript. This is the only raw material the script can use.

  3. 3/7

    Script

    claude -p, through the subscription and with no API, writes the lines and the visuals into roteiro.json and the approval text into legenda.txt, always working from the base.

  4. 4/7

    Check

    checar.py checks that each excerpt in the base is really in the transcript, that the numbers in the lines are spelled out, and that the niche notice and the CTA appear. If it fails, the script goes back once. If it fails again, the video is not made.

  5. 5/7

    Avatar and video

    Nei's avatar comes from the HeyGen studio (subscription). If it fails, the version with a photo and a local voice goes out instead. The stage is a page rendered frame by frame, with counters, word-by-word captions, and the source on screen in every scene.

  6. 6/7

    Publishing

    The video goes to the YouTube channel through yt-pubx and always to the bot on Telegram. The bot receives one of three things: the video, "no topic" (a weak day does not become a video), or "failed at X".

  7. 7/7

    The whole assembly line

    Collection and transcription feed the base; the base limits the script; the check blocks anything without a source; the avatar and the stage assemble the video; publishing always notifies you. Each station is a lesson in this course.

Without a queue and without a gate, automating social media just means posting the wrong thing faster. That is why this is step 4: it uses the terminal, the assistant, and the queue from the previous steps.

02 · Which tool does what

Tap each one. All of them are open INEMA repositories.

1. r7e and r7ex

You hand over one strong idea and out comes a 30-to-40-second 9:16 reel, in the 7-step trigger script, from the hook with a number to the request for a comment.

r7e (reel7etapas) gave the script; r7ex is the new standard (10/18/2026), with a TV-news finish. Lesson 2 of this course.

Input → outputOne thesis of the day → one reel with an avatar, panels timed to the speech, and "comenta PALAVRA" at the end.

2. promoavatar

One topic becomes a script for each of 12 audiences (young people, 40+, entrepreneurs, educators…), each with its own trigger and its own channel.

The bot writes and stops: you approve before spending avatar and render. Lesson 6.

Input → output"/promoavatar <assunto>" → 12 scripts, 2 gates, 12 reels delivered to each audience's channel.

3. promoavatar3

The same topic yields three scripts for the same audience: reach (getting shared), authority (getting saved), and promotional (connecting pain to solution).

Recommended publishing order: reach → authority → promotional. Lesson 6.

Input → output"| alvos=jovens-alc,jovens-aut,jovens-pro" → three videos for young people, with different CTAs.

4. The radars (xviral)

They watch what the biggest names in the niche published, check every number against the transcript, and publish one short a day, with the source on screen and the niche disclaimer.

Finance, health, and law use the same engine; only the channel list, the rules, and the schedule change. Lessons 3, 4, 5, and 7.

Input → outputTimer from Monday to Friday → a 40 to 60 s video with what they said yesterday and the day before, or "no topic".

Example from the repository
  • finviral: finance, Top 10 of the Anbima FInfluence ranking, runs at 02:00, disclaimer "this is not investment advice".
  • saudeviral: health, the most-watched doctors, nutritionists, and psychologists, runs at 05:00, disclaimer "does not replace a medical consultation" and a NO CONSENSUS stamp.
  • advviral: law, 10 lawyers on YouTube, runs at 08:00, disclaimer "does not replace advice from a lawyer".

Collection by transcript, base.md with a literal excerpt, script by claude -p on the subscription, checking by checar.py, avatar by the studio, frame 0 with the date and the strongest number, a comment hook, and a CTA to inema.club.

The schedules are different so they don't compete for the GPU and HeyGen.
